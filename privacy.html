Privacy Policy – Max Videos

Max Videos is a private tool used only by its owner to upload their own videos to their own TikTok account.

What data is used: When the owner logs in with TikTok, the app receives an access token and basic profile information (display name, open ID) via TikTok Login Kit.

How it is used: The token is used only to upload the owner's own videos as drafts to their TikTok inbox.

Storage: The token is stored only locally on the owner's computer. No data is sent to any other server, sold, or shared with third parties.

Deletion: Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. The locally stored token can be deleted at any time.
